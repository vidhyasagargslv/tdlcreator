Load in Tally

  1. Save the .tdl on the Tally PC.
  2. F1 > TDL & Add-On > Manage Local TDLs.
  3. Load TDL on startup: Yes; add the full file path.
  4. Check status says Loaded; open Gateway > your menu.
  5. Backend must answer <ENVELOPE><STATUS>1</STATUS></ENVELOPE> for success.
